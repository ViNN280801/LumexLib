Detailed Description

Every general-purpose type trait of LumexLib, one sub-namespace of lumex::core::utility::traits per topic: meta, invoke, stream, range, string, tuple, value, enums, numeric.

Traits that depend on a module's own types (for example the formatter traits of core/fmt) stay in that module.
