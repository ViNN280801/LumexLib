Detailed Description

is_contiguous_iterator for the iterator classes of the standard libraries below C++20.

From C++20 the trait is std::contiguous_iterator and this header adds nothing. Before, the language cannot tell the iterator of a std::vector from the iterator of a std::deque, so the iterator classes that wrap a raw pointer are named here, one library at a time:

  • libstdc++: __gnu_cxx::__normal_iterator<Pointer, Container> (the iterators of std::vector and std::basic_string);
  • libc++: std::__wrap_iter<Iterator> (std::vector, std::basic_string, and std::array and std::basic_string_view before the bounded iterators), std::__bounded_iter<Iterator> and std::__static_bounded_iter<Iterator, Size> (the hardened modes of libc++);
  • the standard library of MSVC: std::_Vector_iterator, std::_Vector_const_iterator, std::_String_iterator, std::_String_const_iterator, std::_Array_iterator, std::_Array_const_iterator and, under _HAS_CXX17, std::_String_view_iterator<Traits> (the iterator of std::basic_string_view). Each of them holds a pointer (or, for the bounded ones of libc++, an iterator that is contiguous itself) and moves it by pointer arithmetic, so *it and *(it + n) are n elements apart: the promise the trait makes. A wrapper counts as contiguous exactly when what it wraps is. The iterators of std::vector<bool>, std::deque and std::list, reverse iterators, the iterators of the checked debug mode of libstdc++ (_GLIBCXX_DEBUG) and any iterator class of the user are not named here and stay not contiguous; a program names them by a specialization of is_contiguous_iterator, which also overrides this header. The pointer iterators of std::array and std::basic_string_view in libstdc++, and the begin and end of a std::valarray and of a std::initializer_list, are raw pointers and need nothing. The names of the standard libraries are private to them: the parts follow the declarations of their headers, and the tests of this library compile the part for MSVC with cl.exe and clang-cl.
