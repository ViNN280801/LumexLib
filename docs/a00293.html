Detailed Description

The tags and the placeholder type that Expected uses: in_place, unexpect and Unit.

in_place_tag and in_place request in-place construction of the value, unexpect_t and unexpect in-place construction of the error, as std::in_place (C++17) and std::unexpect (C++23) do. Unit is the empty object that fills the success alternative of Expected<void, E>. The is_expected traits are not here but in LumexTypeTraits.hpp. The tags are also visible at global scope.
