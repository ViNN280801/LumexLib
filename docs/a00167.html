Detailed Description

CPU and RAM of single processes, by process ID or by executable name, with the processes they started.

LumexProcessMonitor samples processes on request: each sample gives the process's resident and private memory, its total CPU time, and its share of the whole machine's CPU since the monitor's previous sample of the same process. By default the processes below the asked one (its children, their children, ...) are added to the sample, and the breakdown by process can be asked for. Windows and Linux; elsewhere every query reports process_query_error::unsupported.
