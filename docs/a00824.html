Detailed Description

dwcas_value_t, the 16-byte value of a dwcas_word: two 64-bit halves.

A plain aggregate that is passed and returned by value. lo is the half at the lower address (the one rax carries in cmpxchg16b), hi the half at the higher address (rdx). Equality compares both halves. Declared only where the layer exists (LUMEX_ATOMIC_HAS_DWCAS).
