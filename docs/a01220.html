Detailed Description

Smart pointer module: shared_ptr, weak_ptr, enable_shared_from_this, make_shared, allocate_shared, the pointer casts, owner_less, bad_weak_ptr and the explicit conversions to and from std::shared_ptr, with the module's own control block.

Link lumex::smart_ptr (header-only). Works from C++11. The classes have the interface of the C++17 std::shared_ptr family (arrays included; the array forms of make_shared of C++20 are not provided; unique () is not provided) but are never aliases of the standard types: the control block packs the strong counter and a debt ledger into one 64-bit atomic word and a weak ledger into another, which the split-count engine of core/atomic needs. The names are in lumex::core::smart_ptr and are not injected into the global namespace. The umbrella includes shared/LumexSharedPtr.hpp, weak/LumexWeakPtr.hpp, shared/LumexEnableSharedFromThis.hpp, make/LumexMakeShared.hpp, shared/LumexBadWeakPtr.hpp and interop/LumexSmartPtrStd.hpp; the control blocks and the counters are in ctl/ and the helpers (the detail::access friend, the traits, the configuration macros) in detail/.
