Detailed Description

Time module: LumexTime, the current date and time as text and timestamps in units from nanoseconds to years, and LumexTimer, a stopwatch with helpers that time a callable (LUMEX_MEASURE_TIME).

Link lumex::time (compiled library). Works from C++11. The umbrella includes clock/LumexTime.hpp and timer/LumexTimer.hpp.
