Detailed Description

Expected module: expected<T, E>, a value or an error after the C++23 std::expected, with its void specialization, unexpected, bad_expected_access and the success / failure markers.

Link lumex::expected (header-only; it brings lumex::utility for LUMEX_ASSERT). Works from C++11. The umbrella includes the error types (error/) and the result types (result/); result/ExpectedDetail.hpp and result/ExpectedStorage.hpp are internal. The surface follows std::expected ([expected.expected] of the working draft): the special member functions are deleted, left out and trivial as the standard says, the members are constexpr as far as the language of the standard in use allows, and the differences that remain are listed at the class expected.

Global names: expected, bad_expected_access, make_unexpected, unexpect, unexpect_t and in_place_tag are also visible at global scope. unexpected and in_place are not (the MinGW runtime declares a global function unexpected, and optional has its own in_place): write lumex::core::expected::error::unexpected and lumex::core::expected::result::in_place.
