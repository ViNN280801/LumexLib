Detailed Description

Math module: mathematical constants as LUMEX_MATH_CONSTANTS_* macros, and numeric helpers such as NaN and infinity checks, checked narrowing casts, and the average, RMS and RMSE of a range.

Link lumex::math (header-only). Works from C++11; from C++20 the range helpers also take std::views adaptors. The umbrella includes constants/LumexMathConstants.hpp and ops/LumexMath.hpp.
