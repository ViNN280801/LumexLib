40/**
41 * @file LumexUtility
42 * @brief Utility module, the foundation of the library: assertion, attribute,
43 * keyword and constant macros, compiler, feature and OS checks, type traits,
44 * stack text and core dumps, demangling, safe numeric comparison (with the
45 * ordering classes of its three-way result), bit and byte order helpers, the
46 * iterator range, the process id, a callback slot, the `identity` and `less`
47 * function objects and the `index_sequence` family.
48 * @details Link <tt>%lumex::utility</tt> (compiled library; it links
49 * <tt>%lumex::math</tt>, <tt>%lumex::optional</tt> and <tt>%lumex::span</tt>).
50 * Works from C++11. The umbrella also includes `bit/`, `cast/`, `mem/` and
51 * `ranges/`. In them `count_leading_zeros`, `byte_swap`, `is_little_endian`,
52 * `downcast`, `downcast_noexcept` and `bad_down_cast`, `get_nearest_to`,
53 * `iterator_range` and `mem::as` (reads a value out of raw bytes; the result
54 * is the optional of `lumex::optional` in every standard, `mem::optional_t`,
55 * which converts to and from `std::optional` from C++17) work from C++11.
56 * `functional/` (`functional::identity`, `functional::less`) and `sequence/`
57 * (`sequence::integer_sequence`, `index_sequence`, `make_integer_sequence`,
58 * `make_index_sequence`, `index_sequence_for`) work from C++11: they are the
59 * classes of this library in every standard, never aliases of `std::identity`,
60 * `std::less<void>` or the standard integer sequences (they convert to and
61 * from those where the standard has them). `traits::meta` has the C++11 forms
62 * `is_pointer_to_class`, `is_lvalue_ref_to_class`, `is_complete_type`,
63 * `preserves_cv`, `is_derived_from`, `is_extractible` and `is_byte_like` of
64 * its concepts, and `traits::string::is_string_convertible` the form of
65 * `StringLike`.
66 */
