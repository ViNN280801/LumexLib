Detailed Description

C++11 implementation of std::optional (C++17) and of its C++23 monadic operations: a type-safe nullable value container.

optional<T> holds a T or nothing. The class is the module's own on every standard and is never an alias of std::optional: from C++17 it converts implicitly to and from std::optional<T> (the same T) and compares with it. The storage is a union { dummy, value } followed by the engaged flag, so sizeof equals that of std::optional, and a chain of class templates (see LumexOptionalDetail.hpp), whose last member is the only data member of optional, gives it the special member functions of the standard: trivially copy/move constructible, trivially copy/move assignable and trivially destructible when T is, and a copy or move that is deleted (or ignored, for a move) when T cannot do it. Constructors, the observers and the trivial operations are constexpr from C++11; the operations that change the active member of the union (emplace, assignment, non-trivial reset and swap) are constexpr from C++20 (LUMEX_OPTIONAL_CONSTEXPR_CXX20).

As std::optional, a moved-from optional keeps its (moved-from) value, the assignment between two engaged optionals assigns the value, and a throwing T leaves the engaged state as the standard describes ([optional.assign]). The converting constructors and assignments, the explicit rules and the monadic operations and_then, transform, or_else follow [optional.ctor], [optional.assign] and [optional.monadic]; the monadic operations are SFINAE-constrained (C++11 has no requires): a call whose function is not invocable, whose and_then result is not an optional or whose transform result cannot be held, is not viable instead of failing a static_assert. lumex_bad_optional_access derives from std::logic_error; std::bad_optional_access exists only from C++17 (see LumexOptionalTags.hpp). This header declares everything in lumex::core::optional::opt and puts no name at global scope; the global aliases (optional, nullopt, make_optional, lumex_bad_optional_access) are in LumexOptionalGlobals.hpp, which the umbrella LumexOptional includes.
