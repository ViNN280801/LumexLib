Detailed Description

Utility module, the foundation of the library: assertion, attribute, keyword and constant macros, compiler, feature and OS checks, type traits, stack text and core dumps, demangling, safe numeric comparison (with the ordering classes of its three-way result), bit and byte order helpers, the iterator range, the process id, a callback slot, the identity and less function objects and the index_sequence family.

Link lumex::utility (compiled library; it links lumex::math, lumex::optional and lumex::span). Works from C++11. The umbrella also includes bit/, cast/, mem/ and ranges/. In them count_leading_zeros, byte_swap, is_little_endian, downcast, downcast_noexcept and bad_down_cast, get_nearest_to, iterator_range and mem::as (reads a value out of raw bytes; the result is the optional of lumex::optional in every standard, mem::optional_t, which converts to and from std::optional from C++17) work from C++11. functional/ (functional::identity, functional::less) and sequence/ (sequence::integer_sequence, index_sequence, make_integer_sequence, make_index_sequence, index_sequence_for) work from C++11: they are the classes of this library in every standard, never aliases of std::identity, std::less<void> or the standard integer sequences (they convert to and from those where the standard has them). traits::meta has the C++11 forms is_pointer_to_class, is_lvalue_ref_to_class, is_complete_type, preserves_cv, is_derived_from, is_extractible and is_byte_like of its concepts, and traits::string::is_string_convertible the form of StringLike.
