The common name: std::atomic<std::shared_ptr<T>> from C++11 on.

An alias template of atomic_shared_ptr_lock_free<T> where the lock-free engine exists (and LUMEX_ATOMIC_SMART_PTR_FORCE_LOCK_BASED is not defined), of atomic_shared_ptr_lock_based<T> otherwise; never of the _std_backed wrapper. Being an alias template it cannot be forward declared, partially specialized or befriended: name the engine to do that.
