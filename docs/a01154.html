Detailed Description

make_hazard_pointer_batch and clear_hazard_pointer_batch, which fill and empty many holders at once ([saferecl.hp.holder.nonmem], P3428R4).

make_hazard_pointer_batch (hps) gives every empty element of hps a hazard slot and leaves the non-empty ones alone. It either succeeds for all of them or throws std::bad_alloc with no effect. The module takes all slots from the engine in one call, which is cheaper than one make_hazard_pointer per element. clear_hazard_pointer_batch (hps) ends the protection of every non-empty element and empties it; it cannot fail.

The span is the module's lumex::core::span::view::span, which converts from std::span from C++20.
