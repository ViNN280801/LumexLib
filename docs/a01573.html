Writes the view to a stream as the standard one does.

A formatted output function: the field is width() wide (the view is padded with fill() on the left, or on the right when adjustfield is left), width is reset to 0 after the write, a stream that does not pass the sentry is left untouched, a failing stream buffer sets badbit and an exception of the buffer is handled as the standard says.
